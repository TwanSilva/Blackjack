Rules: 8 decks, dealer peeks, double after split, late surrender. H17 is the default table; S17 is shown because the
Illustrious 18 and Fab 4 are usually quoted for S17. Rounding: floor. Shoe depth: 4 decks remaining, unless noted.

The memory column is the table in `src/engine/strategy/deviations.ts`, which was typed from memory and is **unverified**.
The computed columns come from the EV engine, so they're consistent with the rest of the trainer. They are still a
model, not a published source (see "Method and limits").

## Method and limits

1. **Shoe at a true count:** the remaining shoe at true count t is the average shoe for that count. Low cards
   (2–6) are taken out and high cards (10s, aces) put in, in proportion, until the shoe's Hi-Lo count is −t × decks.
2. **Evaluating the plays:** both plays of each row are evaluated on that shoe with the same total-dependent EV
   calculation as the chart, over the two-card hands that make the total.
3. **Index from the crossing:** the index is the lowest whole number whose floor band favours the deviation on
   average, treating real counts as evenly spread inside a band.

Known reasons a published, simulation-based index can differ by about one:
- **Average shoe:** real shoes at a given count vary around the average used here.
- **Two-card hands only:** real hands include three-card and longer totals. This matters most for 16 vs 10:
  multi-card 16s favour standing, which pulls the published index down toward 0.
- **Uneven bands:** real counts are not evenly spread inside a band, and published lists often use other conventions.
- **Edge cases:** rows whose crossing sits near a band edge (12v3 S17 at 1.32, 13v3 S17 at −2.52, 15vA S17 at
  1.56, TTv6 S17 at 4.71) can move by one under a different convention.
